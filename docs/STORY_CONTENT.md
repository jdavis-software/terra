# Story catalogue — ten geological anchors and eighteen human chapters

## Status and rules

The anchor labels, chapter sequence, subjects and date labels below were visually read from the supplied recording. That proves what the reference **displays**, not the accuracy of every historical claim. Proposed Terra headlines are original drafting directions, not quotations from the source. Final body copy, dates, coordinates and source URLs require the content-review tasks before release.

Write approximately 35–65 words per chapter, with one compact contextual label, a 4–10-word headline and one contextual source action. Prefer large light-weight sans-serif type and deliberate line breaks. Use no fabricated quotations or unverified superlatives. Describe societies as contemporary with other societies, not a single ladder of progress. Maintain nuance about Indigenous histories, regional differences and uncertain dates.

Content records must include `id`, explicit `order`, `displayDate`, `dateKind`, optional numeric date/range in its declared convention, `headline`, `body`, `regionLabel`, `cameraPreset`, `overlayIds`, `sourceIds`, `uncertaintyNote`, `appearance`, and `reviewStatus`. Source IDs resolve to exact primary-source records; pending records fail release content validation. Camera targets are presentation instructions, not claims of an exact birthplace or historical boundary.

## 4. Chapter acts and visual grammar

Group C01–C03 as human beginnings, C04–C13 as settlements/networks, and C14–C18 as connected modern transformations. These grouping boundaries are Terra's explicit editorial choice; the recording displays group labels, but their exact internal boundaries were not independently tested.

Current sites receive a small soft pulse or halo. Earlier story sites can remain as dim markers. Regions are translucent and explicitly illustrative. Arcs indicate a story relationship, not measured traffic volume. No modern political-border layer is required. Backward seeking must remove future sites and modern city light emission.

The floating human-footprint card must include functional toggles for current story regions and earlier story sites, plus a readable limitation: `Illustrative story geography — not borders or population density.` It should not become an analytics panel.
